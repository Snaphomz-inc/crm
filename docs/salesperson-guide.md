# Salesperson walkthrough

Everything you need is under **CRM › My buyers**, worked top to bottom.

1. **My new leads**: buyers who have not been contacted yet. First contact is due 15
   staffed minutes after the inquiry; your manager is alerted at 30.
2. **My follow-ups today**: everything due before the end of your day (overdue in red).
3. **My overdue leads** and **My leads missing next action**: clear both before you finish
   the day, or record why and reschedule.
4. **Waiting for buyer**: you reached out and they have not replied yet.

## On a buyer

* Check **Buyer timezone** (the Log outcome window shows their local time) before calling.
* Call with Dialpad as usual.
* After every interaction press **Log outcome**: pick what happened, write a short summary
  of what was said and agreed, and set the next action with its due time.
  * *No answer* / *Voicemail* proposes a retry on the next staffed day. After three
    unanswered attempts it suggests a nurture review.
  * *Spoke* / *Requested callback* marks the buyer **Connected**.
  * *Do not contact* closes the buyer, stops sales emails and keeps them suppressed.
* Marking a task done is not the same as logging a conversation. Use Log outcome.
* **Qualify** when you have spoken with the buyer:
  * *Ready now* turns the buyer into an opportunity in **Qualified pipeline**.
  * *Needs preparation* / *Exploring* moves them to nurture with a reason and review date.
  * *Unqualified* closes them with a reason.
* In the pipeline, Consultation booked needs the consultation time; BRA signed needs the
  agreement ID. Bookings and signatures from the website update this automatically.
* The **Source** tab shows where the buyer first came from; **Inquiries** lists every inquiry.
* To email a buyer use **Send message** in the chatter. **Log note** is internal only.
